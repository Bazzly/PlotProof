The story behind PlotProof

A survey plan looks official. It doesn't mean it's right.

In Abuja, two brothers each held a survey plan for the land their father left them — signed, stamped, seemingly legitimate. What neither of them knew was that a surveying error decades earlier had drawn their boundaries over the same ground. By the time they discovered it, contractors were already on site and a dispute was already brewing.

Stories like this play out across Nigeria every day — not because people are careless, but because checking a boundary against its neighbors has always required a surveyor, weeks of waiting, and money most families don't have to spare on "just in case."

Technology finds the error in minutes. Protecting your land still takes care — we just make the first part easy.
