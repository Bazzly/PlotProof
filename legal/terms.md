## 2. What PlotProof is - and isn't

PlotProof is an **automated preliminary screening tool**. It checks coordinates you provide (typed manually or extracted from an uploaded survey plan) against a set of known plot boundaries and reports a Low/Medium/High risk estimate.

PlotProof is **not** a certified survey, a legal opinion, or a substitute for a licensed surveyor's professional judgment. Coordinate extraction from uploaded documents (OCR, CRS detection, boundary reconstruction from bearings/distances or drawing geometry) is automated and can be wrong. Results reflect only the data on record at the time of your check - they are not a guarantee that a plot is free of disputes, and a "Low risk" or "no conflict" result **can change** as more plots are added to the system.

**Do not rely solely on PlotProof's output for a legal, financial, or transactional decision about land.** Always engage a certified surveyor for a professional assessment before finalizing a property transaction.
